ABOUT NEXA CLOUD

Practical technology for growing businesses.

Nexa Cloud, also known as Hawassa Business Cloud, is an Ethiopia-based software initiative developing practical cloud tools for everyday business management.

The product focuses on connecting the records that owners and teams work with regularly: products and stock, sales, purchases, expenses, cashbook activity, customer and supplier balances, available approvals, search and reporting.

We believe useful business software should be evaluated against real work. We welcome questions, product walkthroughs and carefully scoped pilot conversations so businesses and potential partners can understand the current capabilities before making a decision.

GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

Understand the product before you commit.

We want prospective users and partners to know what Nexa Cloud is, what it is designed to do, and what should be confirmed in a walkthrough.

Ask us a question ↗
What is Nexa Cloud?

Nexa Cloud (also known as Hawassa Business Cloud) is an Ethiopia-based, web-accessible business-management initiative focused on organizing day-to-day records for shops and small business teams.

What can the platform help manage?

Depending on the current setup, the platform includes workflows for products and stock, sales, purchases, expenses, cashbook activity, customer and supplier balances, approvals, search, reports and operational review.

Who should explore it?

It may be relevant to shop owners, retailers, product-based businesses and teams that need to keep daily transactions, stock and balances organized. A walkthrough is the best way to check whether it fits a specific business.

Can an organization outside Ethiopia discuss partnership?

Yes. Nexa Cloud welcomes exploratory conversations with international software companies, business organizations and implementation partners. Availability, support, local requirements and any commercial terms should be discussed directly; no partnership is implied by this invitation.

How do I request a walkthrough or discuss price?

Email nexacloud.et@gmail.com with your organization, country, business use case and the kind of discussion you want. Current capabilities, availability, scope and commercial terms should be confirmed directly before proceeding.

Does this website submit a form or collect business data?

No online inquiry form is embedded here. The contact links open your email application. Do not send passwords, private customer records or sensitive financial data to the public email address.
