ABOUT NEXA CLOUD

Practical technology for growing businesses.

Nexa Cloud, also known as Hawassa Business Cloud, is an Ethiopia-based software initiative developing practical cloud tools for everyday business management.

The platform focuses on connecting the records that owners and teams work with regularly: products and stock, sales, purchases, expenses, cashbook activity, customer and supplier balances, available approvals, search and reporting.

We welcome questions, product walkthroughs and carefully scoped pilot conversations so businesses and potential partners can understand current capabilities before making a decision.

YOUR BUSINESS. YOUR RECORDS.

Your business data stays private.

Nexa Cloud is designed to help business owners manage records with confidence. Your business information should be accessible only to you and users authorized for your shop.

🔒 PRIVATE BUSINESS RECORDS

Your records are for your business.

Business records are intended to be available to the owner and authorized users within the relevant shop, not to other businesses using Nexa Cloud.

✓ AUTHORIZED ACCESS

Access should follow permissions.

Nexa Cloud is designed around shop-specific access and user roles, helping keep each business’s records separate from other shops’ records.

⌁ SUPPORT WITH PERMISSION

Support access is not automatic.

Nexa Cloud support staff cannot open your business data unless you grant temporary access for a specific support issue. Share access only when needed and review the request before approving it.

Our commitment: privacy and responsible access matter. No software can promise zero risk, so keep passwords secure, authorize only trusted users, and contact us if you have a concern about access to your records.

GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

Understand the product before you commit.

We want prospective users and partners to know what Nexa Cloud is, what it is designed to do, and what should be confirmed in a walkthrough.

Ask us a question ↗
What is Nexa Cloud?

Nexa Cloud is an Ethiopia-based business software initiative bringing together supported records such as products, stock, sales, purchases, expenses, cashbook activity, customer and supplier balances, and reports.

Who is it designed to help?

It is designed around shops, retailers, owners and small teams that want to organize business records and review daily operations in a connected workspace.

Can I try the application?

Visit the application using the Open application link. Access and available features may depend on account setup and authorization.

Can an organization outside Ethiopia discuss partnership?

Yes. We welcome exploratory conversations with organizations in Ethiopia and other markets. A discussion does not mean a partnership already exists.

Can other businesses or Nexa Cloud support staff see my business records?

Your shop records are intended to be accessible only to you and users authorized for your shop, not to other businesses using Nexa Cloud. Support staff cannot open your business data unless you grant temporary access for a specific support issue. Access controls should always be used carefully, and no software can guarantee zero risk. For questions about privacy, read our privacy information or contact Nexa Cloud. This public website is informational; contact links open your email application. Do not email passwords or confidential records.
