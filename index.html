Explore collaboration ↗ Nexa Cloud company logoETHIOPIA · BUILT FOR EVERYDAY BUSINESS ABOUT NEXA CLOUD Practical technology for growing businesses. Nexa Cloud, also known as Hawassa Business Cloud, is an Ethiopia-based software initiative developing practical cloud tools for everyday business management. The platform focuses on connecting the records that owners and teams work with regularly: products and stock, sales, purchases, expenses, cashbook activity, customer and supplier balances, available approvals, search and reporting. We welcome questions, product walkthroughs and carefully scoped pilot conversations so businesses and potential partners can understand current capabilities before making a decision.
