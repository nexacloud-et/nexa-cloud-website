ABOUT NEXA CLOUD

Practical technology for growing businesses.

Nexa Cloud, also known as Hawassa Business Cloud, is an Ethiopia-based software initiative focused on helping businesses organize their everyday operations through cloud-based tools.

We are working toward a practical business-management experience that brings key records—such as products, sales, purchases, expenses and balances—into one place for owners and their teams.

Our approach is grounded in learning from real business workflows and improving the product through careful testing and feedback.
